## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-08-01 | Information Disclosure / Elevation of Privilege | EdgeLLM._call_llama_server 目標 URL | high | mitigate | base URL 一律取自 config.LLM_SERVER_HOST（預設 loopback 127.0.0.1，08-01 設定），不硬編對外位址；實際綁定範圍由 08-05 裝置外部 curl 驗證 |
